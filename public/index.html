MEA (Managerial Economics) Concepts Powered by This Backend

How this real-world lifestyle tool directly implements core syllabus economics.

1. Purchasing Power Parity (PPP)

Exchange rates alone do not reflect real living standards. PPP compares what an identical basket of goods costs across two economies.

PPP Rate = Target Basket ÷ Origin Basket

2. Real vs. Nominal Income

A ₹60 LPA salary abroad sounds huge nominally, but after higher rent and 28% tax, real savings can sometimes be lower than ₹15 LPA in India.

Real Savings = Gross × (1 − Tax) − Basket

3. Live Foreign Exchange Rate

Our Node.js Express backend fetches live daily currency rates via REST API and caches them server-side so conversions stay accurate globally.

Nominal Value = Salary × (Rate_To / Rate_From)

4. Opportunity Cost of Migration

Compares 5-year accumulated wealth in INR across two cities so graduates weigh financial trade-offs rationally.

5-Yr Net Gain = 60 × (Savings_B − Savings_A)
